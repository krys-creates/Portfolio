// DIGITAL MARKETING STRATEGY & BRAND DESIGN DEVELOPMENT PROJECT (MAY-AUG 2025)

# role:

audience & media researcher, digital media strategist, brand identity & graphics designer

# tools:

Adobe Illustrator & Photoshop

# client:

Faculty of Science and Engineering (FSE) Maynooth University

/ project brief...

This project delivered an end-to-end marketing strategy and brand identity to launch and grow the Faculty's presence on LinkedIn and Instagram, positioning FSE as a distinct, engaging voice within the wider Maynooth University landscape.

< PHASE 01: COMPETITIVE BENCHMARKING >

Auditing FSE's own sub-departments, Maynooth University's core and umbrella accounts; 5 leading STEM institutions across Ireland (Trinity, UCD, TUD) and abroad (MIT, Cambridge) on LinkedIn and Instagram.

↗ result

A content playbook — research-led storytelling, consistent hashtag systems, alumni story formats, data-driven recap posts — adapted specifically to FSE's voice and audience.

< PHASE 02: TARGET AUDIENCE RESEARCH >

6 audiences — Irish & international prospective/current students, mature students, industry partners, parents, schools — mapping each to its primary platform & key messaging priorities.

↗ result

‘Audience-platform-message’ matrix that guides every content decision.

< PHASE 03: DIGITAL CONTENT STRATEGY >

Combined benchmarking insights with audience research into a 12-month content calendar covering 20+ FSE’s yearly events & including content that can be posted anytime to keep digital presence stably active.

↗ result

A ready-to-execute yearly calendar with format recommendations, sample captions, visual direction and technical execution notes for every entry.

< PHASE 04: BRAND & SOCIAL MEDIA DESIGN >

Designed FSE's logo and a full suite of social media templates. See visuals below!

< PHASE 05: REPORT >

Presenting the end-to-end, ready for implementation digital strategy to Faculty stakeholders.
