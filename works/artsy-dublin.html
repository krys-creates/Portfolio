/ Artsy Dublin is...

a web platform (laptop & phone adapted) for discovering Dublin’s local performance and arts events — such as music, theatre, film, and more — with personalised recommendations based on users’ interests. It combines event discovery with lightweight social connection, helping people find what’s worth seeing and others to experience it with.

< PHASE 01: UX
RESEARCH >

the users are

Dublin's art-goers who want to stay on top of art and performance that actually suits their taste
Solo attendees who want to share cultural experiences but don't have a ready-made group to go with

they need

a single trustworthy place to browse cultural events they can actually rely on, recommendations that reflect their real interests, and a low-pressure way to find company for an event without committing to a long-term group

but they encounter

general-purpose platforms (such as Eventbrite) that fall short for this niche:

i/ inaccurate tagging — user-generated events carry wrong or misleading category labels

ii/ weak recommendation algorithms — no nuanced interest profile is built

iii/ no spontaneous social layer — existing social features are built for long-term groups, not lightweight, event-specific buddy-finding

< PHASE 02: CREATING SOLUTION >

// search & discovery
  • sorting events by genre and time, sourced live from reputable venue/institution APIs
  • clear, reliable information sourced from APIs replaces user-generated tagging
// personalisation
  • art event diary that is formulated by logging attended events with a reflection, rating, and photo; doubles as a keepsake and the basis for the recommendation profile
  • tailored “For You” feed where recommendations are built from interest profile, diary history, and what connections are attending
// social layer
  • messaging between users
  • posting, such as sharing updates & reflections tied to events; interacting with posts, such as commenting & liking
  • event buddy matching — user sees who else is going to an event and sends a “join me” request, no long-term commitment required

< PHASE 03: DESIGNING APPEARANCE >

WARM & TACTILE & CULTURAL — not another cold tech app or generic ticketing platform
