/ project brief...

an interactive campus navigation platform (prototype, laptop adapted) designed to address the functional and aesthetic limitations of Trinity's legacy website, reimagined as an accessible, centralized building information hub.

< PHASE 01: UX
RESEARCH >

the users are

local & international students, staff, tourists

they need

  • quick, centralized access to accurate, up-to-date buildings, venues & facilities information, without need to search for information externally
  • accessible, simple & visually pleasing interface
  • ability to quickly identify needed landmarks

but they encounter

  • limited, outdated information, which forces users to waste time looking for & cross-referencing information externally (other websites, social media, peers & staff)
  • cluttered, inaccessible website layout, harsh UI elements & broken interaction flows

< PHASE 02: DEFINING STRATEGY TO REFRESH THE OLD PLATFORM >

i/ information architecture & enhanced ways to search

Two ways to search updated information:

  • Interactive Map for visual search
  • A-Z catalogue for textual search

Other: rich set of photos & detailed entrance markers to ease orientation. Personalized search based on user category (tourist, staff, student). Save function.

ii/ accessibility

Accessibility settings included & WCAG compliance.

iii/ refreshing interface design

Overall direction: shift away from ‘cold’ & harsh utility tool look

// other elements

iv/ developing with effortless data scalability in mind

All building data is centralized in a single, clean file, allowing administrators to add, update, or remove buildings without coding knowledge, ensuring the platform scales dynamically as Trinity's campus infrastructure evolves.
