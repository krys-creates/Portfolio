/ Gapminder is...

an interactive bubble chart that shows how wealth, health and population relate across 187 countries in 2018. Each bubble is a country: its position shows income and life expectancy, its size shows population and its colour shows world region. I designed it to be explored with a mouse, a keyboard or a screen reader, and it is adapted to both laptop and phone screens. A second view colours each country by whether people live longer or shorter than its income predicts.

< DESIGN
CHOICES >

  • Position, size, colour: income (x) and life expectancy (y) place each country, population sets the bubble size and region sets the colour.
  • Glow and focus: hovering a bubble brings it to full brightness, dims the rest and shows a pop-up with its details.
  • Two views: colour by region, or by years above or below what income predicts, with a trend line and the biggest outliers labelled.
  • Quiet background: a faint 2018 watermark, subtle grid lines and a region legend keep the data in front.

< ACCESSIBILITY
CONSIDERED >

  • Keyboard navigation: arrow keys move between countries; Home and End jump to the poorest and richest.
  • Search: type a country name to highlight it.
  • Interactive legend: legend items are real buttons that focus a region by mouse, touch or keyboard.
  • Screen reader support: text description and announced selections.
  • Data table: open it to read exact values and find countries without relying on colour.
  • Light and dark mode: one toggle for comfortable reading.

< TECH
IMPLEMENTATION >

Built in p5.js and plain JavaScript:

  • Object-oriented: each country is its own object holding its data, position, radius and colour, which keeps the draw loop clean.
  • From CSV: the sketch reads the dataset, finds the minimum and maximum of each value and uses them to place and size every bubble.
