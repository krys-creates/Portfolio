/ Blizzard Countdown is...

an interactive, gamified story developed in Twine SugarCube, focused on designing clear, meaningful interactions that directly influence narrative progression and outcomes. The project explores how interface design and user decision-making can shape a complex interactive experience.

< synopsis >

A tense, resource-driven interactive narrative where an Expedition Lead (the player) must navigate the final 12 hours of a catastrophic Antarctic blizzard, managing vital resources while balancing logic, empathy, and competing priorities. Through a series of consequential decisions, users engage with ethical and strategic challenges that shape both the experience and its outcome.

The outcome depends on the ethics, efficiency, and logic of the lead’s actions.

< narrative structure >

The structure is complex & consists of 38 nodes (story passages), where Gauntlet & Bottlenecks narrative techniques were used. The image provides a visualisation of the structure.
